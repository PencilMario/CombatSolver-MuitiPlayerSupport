# 0.36.2

## 简体中文

- 修复自动出牌过程中，重复的回合开始通知误触发重算、清空路线并中断执行的问题。手动重新计算仍正常可用。
- 围巾触发的第五张免费牌，现在会在动作胶囊中显示遗物名称与“本张免费”，方便确认触发时机。

## English

- Fixed duplicate turn-start requests triggering unnecessary recalculation during auto-play, clearing the route and interrupting execution. Manual recalculation remains available.
- The fifth card made free by Brilliant Scarf now shows the relic name and “Free card” in its action capsule.
