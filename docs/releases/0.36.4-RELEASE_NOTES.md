# 0.36.4

## 简体中文

- 遗物配置增加 1～3 优先级，3 最高，同等战损下优先保住玩家指定的遗物。
- 增加带骨肉半血目标，可尝试利用战后回血获得更高的最终生命；保留独立开关、血量额度及达标早停。
- 同名敌人增加从左到右的编号，区分千足虫各段；墨之刃生成的小刀显示附魔标注。

- 摘要栏左侧增加“当前路线摘要”小标题，策略结果和血量保持右对齐。

## English

- Added relic priorities from 1 to 3 (3 highest), favoring preferred targets at equal HP cost.
- Added an optional Meat on the Bone half-HP target that accounts for net post-combat healing, with its own switch and HP allowance while preserving target-based early stopping.
- Same-name enemies are numbered from left to right, distinguishing millipede segments. Shivs created by Blade of Ink now show their enchantment.

- Added a "Current route summary" heading on the left, keeping strategy results and HP aligned to the right.
